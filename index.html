Global Benchmarking

How We Compare
Against the Best

Our curriculum was designed after rigorous analysis of programmes at IIT Madras (#1 NIRF), IIIT Hyderabad, IIT Bombay, NUS Singapore, MIT, and Stanford — extracting the finest pedagogical practices.

🎯 Our Differentiation

Unlike most institutions, RGDU integrates Quantum Computing, AGI fundamentals, and Psychological Impact of Technology as core courses from Year 1 — a structure borrowed from MIT's interdisciplinary model and adapted for India's NEP 2020 framework.

Key Finding — IIT Madras

3-Basket PG Structure

Theory + Systems + AI/ML — every student must credit from each basket. We adopted this for M.Sc programmes, ensuring breadth and depth simultaneously.

Key Finding — IIIT-H

EECS Convergence Model

CS and ECE convergence; breadth electives from Year 2 onwards. We embed hardware-software co-design tracks in our B.Tech CSE from Semester 3.

Key Finding — NUS

Common Pool Admission + AI Split

From 2025/26, NUS admits CS + AI students in one pool, splitting in Year 3. We implement this as a Flexible Specialisation System (FSS) from Semester 5.

Self-Paced & Blended Learning Framework

Online (SWAYAM/NPTEL)

Up to 30% of elective credits may be completed via NPTEL/SWAYAM certified MOOCs — fully recognised under ABC.

Hybrid Classroom

Core courses use flipped classroom model — recorded lectures + live problem-solving sessions. Attendance policy 60% for hybrid students.

Industry Co-op (Waterloo Model)

Mandatory 6-week industry immersion in Semester 5. 12-week optional co-op in Semester 7 with partner companies (Amazon, Infosys, ISRO, IBM Quantum).

Credit Transfer & ABC

All earned credits stored in Academic Bank of Credits (DigiLocker-linked). Students may transfer credits to/from any ABC-registered Indian university.

Intelligence Adopted from Global Best

IIT Madras

S/E/H/P/F Credit Classification

Science, Engineering, Humanities, Professional, Free elective credit taxonomy. Ensures holistic education beyond pure CS.

✓ Adopted: Core / Skill / Elective / Research taxonomy
IIIT Hyderabad

IT Workshops from Semester 1

Industry-ready tech workshops embedded from the very first semester so students master current software tools early.

✓ Adopted: IT Workshop I & II in Semesters 1–2
IIIT Hyderabad

Research Exposure from Year 1

Students enter research labs as early as Year 1, turning undergrad into a research-practitioner rather than just a learner.

✓ Adopted: Emerging Tech Seminar + Thesis in B.Sc Hons
IIT Bombay

Lean Core + Maximum Elective Flexibility

Only essential courses are mandatory. Students fill 40% of their credits with free and departmental electives of their choice.

✓ Adopted: 25% open + departmental elective credits
NUS Singapore

Common Pool Admission + Late Specialisation

All CS/AI students share a common first two years, then differentiate. Prevents wrong-branch regret at admission time.

✓ Adopted: FSS — Flexible Specialisation from Sem 5
MIT + Stanford

Quantum Computing as Core Subject

MIT's 18.435J and Stanford's CS259Q are flagship quantum courses. We make Quantum Computing a mandatory core from Sem 4.

✓ Adopted: Quantum Computing core + 3-track advanced pathway
CMU

AI Ethics as a Core Course

Carnegie Mellon introduced a standalone AI degree in 2018 with ethics as a foundational requirement. We follow suit.

✓ Adopted: AI Ethics & Responsible Technology (Sem 4, 3cr)
University of Waterloo

Mandatory Industry Co-op Semester

Waterloo's world-famous co-op programme ensures every graduate has verified industry experience before degree completion.

✓ Adopted: 6-wk mandatory + 12-wk optional co-op
IISc Bangalore

Research Thesis in Undergraduate

IISc's B.Sc Research programme makes undergraduate thesis compulsory. We replicate this for the B.Sc (Hons) exit.

✓ Adopted: 6-credit thesis in B.Sc Hons exit (Year 3)
